Airtable's Vector Search Architecture: Hierarchical Partition Capping in Milvus at Scale
Airtable's semantic search system (backing their Omni AI feature) uses one Milvus partition per customer base for strict isolation, but hit a hard wall at ~100k partitions per collection (partition creation ballooning from 20ms to 250ms). Fix: hierarchical capping at 400 collections × 1,000 partitions per cluster = 400k bases per cluster, with new clusters provisioned as needed. HNSW index selected for 99–100% recall with sub-500ms P99 latency, with DiskANN for cold partitions and hot-loading on demand — a clean worked example of letting data properties drive architecture.
