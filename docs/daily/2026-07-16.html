AI Customer Support Architecture: How Airbnb, Booking.com, and Expedia Handle the Automation Boundary
All three platforms converge on the same structural insight: automation works well for retrieval-based cases (refund status, rebooking) but hits a hard ceiling at adjudication — disputes where competing party claims require judgment, not lookup. Airbnb uses layered intent models plus a refund-ratio predictor trained on historical agent decisions to attempt autonomous resolution; Expedia invests in 30-language conversation summaries to ensure escalated cases arrive with full context intact. The confidence threshold — the single scalar that routes a case to a human — is identified as the highest-leverage tuning parameter in the entire pipeline.
