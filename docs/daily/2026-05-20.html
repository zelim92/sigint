Project Zero: 0-click Pixel 10 exploit chain via Tensor G5 VPU driver MMIO over-mmap
Google Project Zero ported the CVE-2025-54957 Pixel 9 Dolby 0-click to Pixel 10 by replacing the blocked stack-canary overwrite with dap_cpdp_init, then exploited a trivial bounds-check omission in the new Tensor G5 VPU driver (/dev/vpu): vpu_mmap calls remap_pfn_range using caller-supplied VMA size without bounding to the MMIO region, exposing the kernel .text/.data for arbitrary read/write in five lines. Reported Nov 24 2025, patched 71 days later in the February Pixel bulletin — a useful case study in how hardware bring-up introduces regression surface.
