Attested TLS relay attack (CVE-2026-33697, CVSS 7.5): formal verification exposes all seven binding mechanisms in Meta, Edgeless, Cocos AI
TU Dresden used ProVerif to find that all seven attestation-to-TLS binding mechanisms in production TEE deployments (WhatsApp Private Processing, Contrast, Cocos AI) are vulnerable: an attacker with the enclave's ephemeral TLS key can relay genuine attestation evidence while terminating the session at their own endpoint, passing all cryptographic checks. None of the seven mechanisms bind evidence to application traffic. Vendors should confirm binding extends to the client's application traffic key, not just the handshake.
